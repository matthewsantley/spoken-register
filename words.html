Every word in the pool, grouped by the language it is supposed to come from.

What I am actually asking for

I do not speak most of these languages. The words were gathered from reference sources, and none of them has been confirmed by a speaker.

That is not a theoretical worry. While assembling this pool I found two entries that were simply invented — one recorded as Lithuanian for “sky”, one as Greenlandic for “star”. Neither is a real word. Both are gone now, but they were only caught by accident, and there is no reason to assume they were the only two.

So if you speak one of these languages: is the word right? Does it mean what it says? Would it sound odd, or worse, to someone who speaks it? Even one confirmed word is a real contribution. It takes about a minute.

Two ways to tell me, whichever suits. This is right and This is wrong open a pre-filled email — no account needed. The small gh links open a GitHub issue instead, which makes your answer public so nobody has to check the same word twice. Your language missing entirely?
